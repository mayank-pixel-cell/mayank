Focus lockdown

When the urge hits, the shield holds.

During a focus sprint, or after you reach a daily limit, blocked apps and sites are covered by a shield with a live countdown. Pick a normal mode with a deliberate override, or strict mode with none.

  • Block lists you control, with one-click presets.
  • Daily time limits per app that reset at midnight.
  • Strict mode removes every override.
The big idea

One day. Two devices. One honest number.

Most trackers only see the device they’re on. Arc Tracker syncs your phone and laptop over an encrypted connection, so your screen time reflects your whole day.

  • See your phone and laptop totals side by side, then combined.
  • Compare your real total with your daily goal.
  • Each device keeps working offline.

Knowledge Base

Frequently Answered

Essential clarifications on how Arc Tracker protects your focus, syncs privately, and honors the 100% Free Manifesto.

How does the Streak Freeze protection rule work?
Life happens. In alignment with sustainable habit psychology, Arc Tracker includes 1 automated Streak Freeze protection per month. If an emergency, family event, or illness causes you to miss a day, the system forgives that single gap rather than resetting a months-long streak to zero. This preserves your psychological momentum while keeping accountability real.
How does dual-device synchronization work without central servers?
Arc Tracker synchronizes through your own Google account using Google Drive's isolated private appDataFolder. Data is merged client-side via a non-destructive union algorithm so your streaks and accomplishments are never overwritten. Because we run zero centralized company database servers, your routines remain completely private and sovereign.
Why is early exit forbidden in Strict Mode?
Procrastination and impulsive tab-switching thrive on escape hatches. When Strict Mode is active during a focus sprint, standard cancel and close actions are locked down. If temptation strikes, the emergency anti-impulse safeguard requires typing the pledge UNBREAKABLE to override the lock—forcing your prefrontal cortex to re-engage before giving up.
Why is Arc Tracker completely free with zero subscriptions?
In accordance with our 100% Free Manifesto, self-discipline is a fundamental human right, never a $12.99/month luxury subscription. Because Arc Tracker executes 100% locally on your machine via IndexedDB and Web Workers, our server overhead is virtually zero. You receive a complete discipline operating system at zero cost, forever.

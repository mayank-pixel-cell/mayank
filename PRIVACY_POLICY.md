# Privacy Policy for Arc Tracker

**Last updated:** October 25, 2026

**Arc Tracker** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, storage, and usage when you use the Arc Tracker mobile and desktop applications.

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection & Offline-First Design

Arc Tracker is built from the ground up as a **100% offline-first, air-gapped application**:

- **No Personal Information:** We do not collect, request, or store your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information (PII).
- **No Telemetry or Tracking:** We do not include third-party tracking SDKs, analytics frameworks, advertising beacons, or telemetry services.
- **No Remote Servers:** The application operates strictly locally on your device and does not transmit your habits, goals, journal entries, or progress telemetry over the internet.

---

## 2. On-Device Data Storage & Security

All your habit tracking progress, customizable metrics, journal entries, and streak records are stored **exclusively on your local device**:

- Stored using hardware-accelerated local databases (Tauri Plugin Store / IndexedDB).
- Protected with client-side WebCrypto encryption at rest.
- Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use the in-app "Export Data" feature to create a manual backup.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

- Arc Tracker contains **no third-party advertisements**.
- Arc Tracker does not sell, trade, rent, or share any user information with third parties.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy

Because Arc Tracker does not collect any personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

---

## 5. Permissions

Arc Tracker requires no intrusive Android or iOS device permissions:

- No Camera access
- No Location / GPS access
- No Contacts access
- No Microphone access

---

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within this document and reflected with an updated date.

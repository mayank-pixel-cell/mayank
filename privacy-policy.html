Privacy Policy for Arc Tracker

Application: Arc Tracker (Desktop, Android & iOS)
Last Updated: October 25, 2026
Developer: Mayank / Arc Tracker Team

🔒 Plain-English Executive Summary

Arc Tracker is built from the ground up as a 100% offline-first, client-side encrypted application. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or monetize your habits, screen time, or personal data. Everything you do stays exclusively on your own device.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Arc Tracker does not require registration, phone numbers, or credit cards to use its core features.

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or store names, addresses, or phone numbers on any remote servers.
  • No Telemetry or Tracking Frameworks: The application does not bundle Google Analytics, Firebase Tracking, Facebook SDKs, or any third-party behavioral telemetry.
  • No Remote Server Ingestion: Your habit completions, streak logs, daily notes, and screen time records remain private and stored strictly on your local hardware.

2. On-Device Storage & Cryptographic Security

All user data is stored locally using high-durability hardware storage engines:

  • Native Storage: Stored via encrypted IndexedDB and Tauri Plugin Store on your device filesystem.
  • Client-Side Cryptography: Protected with W3C WebCrypto PBKDF2 (100,000 rounds SHA-256) and AES-GCM encryption at rest.
  • Offline Airgap: The application functions completely in Airplane Mode without internet access.

3. Device Permissions & Usage Details

Arc Tracker does not request access to your camera, microphone, GPS location, contacts, or photo library:

  • Screen Time & App Monitoring (Android/Desktop): When enabled by the user, Arc Tracker uses the standard Android PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS system API or local window handles solely to calculate screen time and enforce user-chosen focus lockout barriers. This data never leaves the device.
  • Notifications: Local system notifications are used to alert you when your daily app quota expires or when a Pomodoro focus timer finishes.

4. Account Deletion & Right to Erasure

In accordance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) and Google Play User Data policies, users have complete and immediate control over their data:

  • Instant Factory Reset: You can erase all tracked habits, logs, passwords, and screen time data directly inside the application via Settings → System & Data → Factory Reset (Erase Everything).
  • Zero Lingering Footprint: Because data is not retained on remote servers, uninstalling the app or executing a Factory Reset completely and irrevocably purges all data.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Because Arc Tracker does not collect any personal information whatsoever, it complies fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR-K. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

6. App Store & Platform Compliance

Apple App Store Complies with Guidelines 5.1.1 & 5.1.2 (Data Not Collected)
Google Play Store Complies with Target SDK & Data Safety Zero-Collection Policy
Airgap Standard 100% Offline-First Architecture & Encrypted Backups

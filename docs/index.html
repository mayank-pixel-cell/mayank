Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

Do I need an account or subscription to use Arc Tracker?
No. Arc Tracker is 100% free and open-source. There are no subscriptions, paywalls, or mandatory accounts. You can use all features offline right out of the box.
How does the YouTube & App Blocker work?
During deep focus sprints and Pomodoro sessions, the app blocker restricts high-dopamine social apps and entertainment YouTube channels. It actively whitelists learning creators (e.g. Chai aur Code, CodeWithHarry, FreeCodeCamp) so your study sessions remain productive.
How does multi-device synchronization work without servers?
Arc Tracker uses Google Drive's isolated private appDataFolder. Data is encrypted and merged via a non-destructive union algorithm so your streaks and accomplishments are never overwritten, while remaining completely private to your Google account.
How do I install Arc Tracker on iPhone or iPad?
Open the web app in Safari, tap the Share icon at the bottom, and select "Add to Home Screen". The app will install with custom icons and launch full-screen with offline support.
